Found in the old app: a circular play/pause button floats fixed at bottom-right of every article, letting the user listen to the story instead of reading it. It stayed in the same spot while scrolling, sitting on top of body text. I kept the feature — text-to-speech for articles is genuinely useful for agents reading on the move — but replaced the floating overlay with a dedicated bar at the bottom, matching the same visual language as the Radio mini-player already in the app. Same idea, doesn't sit on top of the words you're trying to read.

SpiceJet Faces Fresh Financial Strain as Pilot Salary Delays Persist, Seeks Government-Backed Loan

GNN Bureau · 9 June 2026, 1:32 PM · 4 min read

New Delhi, June 9: Budget carrier SpiceJet is facing renewed financial challenges, with salary payments to many pilots delayed for several months as the airline seeks emergency funding under a government-backed credit scheme to stabilise operations.

According to internal communications reviewed by Reuters, salary disbursements for a significant number of pilots have been delayed since March, highlighting the airline's ongoing cash flow difficulties.

The airline, once India's second-largest domestic carrier with a market share of nearly 15 percent in 2019, has seen its position weaken considerably. It currently holds around 3.4 percent of the domestic market, ranking fourth among Indian airlines.
